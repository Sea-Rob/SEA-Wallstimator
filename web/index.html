1 · Print the Reference Markers

Download the two-page PDF and print both pages on A4. Tape page 1 (Marker A) at the left end of the wall area and page 2 (Marker B) at the right end, flat against the wall.

Print at 100% / actual size — do not use “fit to page”. (If your printer rescaled anyway, the next step corrects for it.)

Download Reference Marker PDF (2 pages, A4)

2 · Verify print scale

With a tape measure, measure the ruler strip on a printed page from the 0 mark to the … mm mark, and enter what you read — this catches printers that silently rescale.

3 · Capture the wall

Point the camera so a Reference Marker is fully in view, then capture a single frame — or record a pan: start at Marker A (left), sweep slowly and steadily across the wall to Marker B (right), then stop.

4 · Rectified Wall Image — measure to check

The frame below is re-projected flat-on with a real millimetre scale. Tap two points to measure the distance between them — check it against a tape measure on the wall.

5 · Confirm the wall edges and the Floor Line

Drag the blue guides to the left, right and top edges of the wall, and the amber Floor Line to where the wall meets the floor. Pinch to zoom (mouse: scroll) and drag the image to move around for precise placement.

6 · Trace the Obstructions

Mark everything on the wall the product cannot overlap — windows, doors, pipes, meter boxes, vents. Pick a type, press Trace, then drag a rectangle over the obstruction. Tap an outline to select it, drag it to move, drag its corners to resize, or delete it. The red hatched area around an outline is its Exclusion Zone — the clearance the standard requires (600 mm around openable windows and doors), which the product must also stay out of.
